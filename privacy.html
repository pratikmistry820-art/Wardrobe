Wardrobe — Privacy policy

Wardrobe is a personal clothing-inventory app that runs entirely in your browser. There is no Wardrobe server and no account with us.

What the app stores and where

  • On your device: your clothing records and photos are stored in your browser's local storage so the app works offline.
  • In your Google Drive (only if you sign in with Google): the same data is saved in the hidden application data folder of your own Google Drive so it is backed up and available on your other devices. The app can only see files it created in that folder — it cannot see any of your other Drive files.

Google permissions

When you sign in, the app asks for: See, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive (drive.appdata), and your basic profile (name and email address) to show which account is signed in. Access tokens stay in your browser. The app's author never receives your data, photos or tokens.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with anyone. No analytics, no ads, no tracking.

Deleting your data

In the app go to Settings → Danger zone → Delete all my wardrobe data. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions (choose the app, then "Delete all connections"), which also lets you delete its hidden Drive data from Google Drive settings → Manage apps.
